# Mindfull — Mood Check-in 🌤️

A small 2-minute mood check-in experience for OOCA.

This project is designed to help users take a short pause, notice how they feel, and reflect on what is currently on their mind.

Instead of making users name their emotions directly, the experience uses simple choices and the OOCA mascot to guide them through a short reflection.

## About

Sometimes I know that something is bothering me, but I don't really know what I'm feeling or how to explain it.

So I wanted to create a simple experience that doesn't feel like a test.

The experience takes around 2 minutes:

**Check-in → Reflect → Take a small step**

At the end, users get a personalised reflection and one small thing they can try.

## User Flow

1. Choose a language
2. Check in with how your mind feels today
3. Choose what has been taking up space in your thoughts
4. See a reflection based on your choices
5. Try a small next step or explore again

## Design

The design focuses on making the experience feel:

- Simple
- Friendly
- Calm
- Low-pressure
- Easy to use on mobile

The OOCA mascot is used as a friendly companion throughout the experience instead of acting as a therapist or giving a diagnosis.
